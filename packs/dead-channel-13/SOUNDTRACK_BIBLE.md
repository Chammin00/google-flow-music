## Production & Stem Rules for Flow Music

1. **Always export Stems (`drums`, `bass`, `instruments`, `vocals`) + Master (`WAV 48 kHz`)** for `DEAD CHANNEL 13` so `Invoke-DeadChannelScoreMix.ps1` can automatically dip the `vocals` stem (`-30 dB`) whenever character dialogue plays in `EN_DUB` or `JP_SUB`, then bring full brass/bass/drums up during impact frames.
2. **Keep Analog Warmth Authentic:** Prompt for slap bass, Rhodes electric piano, muted trumpet/tenor sax, analog Roland/Juno pads, breakbeat acoustic drums, and tape saturation—never sterile modern EDM drops.
3. **Sync to `cues.json` (`shots.json` Cuts 01–05):** Track `dc13-cue-zero-beat-protocol` is timed directly to the 32-second catwalk brawl (`0–6s`, `6–12s`, `12–18s`, `18–26s`, `26–32s`).
